# Affordable AI for Knowledge Work

*A short guide for learners on a budget. Adapted for this bootcamp from Panaversity's student guide [Affordable Agentic AI Development](https://github.com/panaversity/learn-mcp/blob/main/docs/affordable-agentic-ai.md), 26 November 2026.*

> [!NOTE]
> Prices, plans and features change often. Everything below was written in November 2026. Check each company's own pricing page before you pay, and treat the figures here as a guide, not a quote.

## The short version

**Start free. Pay only when a real need shows up.** You can begin this bootcamp, and do many of the book's labs, with free accounts. Upgrade one app at a time, when a step needs a feature your free plan doesn't have, or when you keep hitting usage limits.

**Panaversity does not provide AI subscriptions or accounts.** You choose, buy and manage your own, and pay any charges yourself.

## What each part of the bootcamp needs

| What | Free account | Paid plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chatting, attaching files, checking answers (Step 00 checks, many book labs) | ✅ Claude and ChatGPT | Not needed |
| Making sheets, documents and slides in the apps | ✅ Mostly, within the free usage limits | Helps with longer jobs |
| Working in a **Claude project** on your own folder (the professional route) | ❌ A free Claude account can't work in your folders. On the free route you work in ordinary chats, attaching files and saving results yourself (Step 00, A9) | Claude **Pro** or higher |
| Scheduled and triggered work, connectors, skills, cloud sessions (steps 3, 5, 6, 7, 13, 20 and 21) | ❌ Usually not: on the free route you run the instruction by hand and label it *simulated*, which earns fewer points | A paid plan in at least one app |
| Repeating each job in the second app | Free works for most steps | Not needed |
| GitHub and GitHub Desktop | ✅ Free | Not needed |

The book's [Set up the apps, "Which plan has what"](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/claude-and-chatgpt-2/new-way-of-working/set-up-the-apps/#which-plan-has-what) keeps an up-to-date list of what each plan includes.

## Ways to keep the cost down

1. **Pay for one app, not two.** You only need a paid plan for the app you do the main work in. Claude Pro is the one this bootcamp is built around. Keep ChatGPT on its free plan for the second run, or write the transfer plan instead, as the book allows.
2. **Use the second app on a free plan.** The free ChatGPT plan is enough to repeat most steps' jobs and compare the results.
3. **Try ChatGPT Go if your country offers it.** It is a low-cost plan with higher limits than the free plan in many countries ([What is ChatGPT Go?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11989085-what-is-chatgpt-go)). Check the price in your own currency at checkout.
4. **Watch for annual billing.** Paying for a year at once can lower the monthly price. Only do this once you are sure you will keep using the app.
5. **Cancel what you don't use.** Set a reminder before each renewal date.
6. **Advanced: the Claude app without a Claude plan.** The Claude desktop app can run on a model from another company, at a lower cost, with some features lost. The book explains how, and what you give up, in [Appendix: the Claude app without a Claude plan](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/appendices/claude-and-chatgpt-2/appendix-the-claude-app-without-a-claude-plan/). It is optional and more technical, so leave it until you are comfortable with the apps.

## Don't share accounts

Splitting one paid account between friends looks cheaper, but Anthropic's [consumer terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms) don't allow sharing your login, and OpenAI's terms are similar. A shared account also mixes up everyone's files, memory and conversations, which is a privacy problem and makes your bootcamp work impossible to grade as yours. Work together through your forks on GitHub, never through a shared account.

## Paying from outside the US

- Check that your card or bank allows international recurring payments.
- Compare the final amount at checkout, including tax, card fees and the exchange rate.
- Never buy through unofficial resellers or "shared" accounts.

## A one-minute decision

1. **Start Step 00 on free accounts.** Its checks work without a paid plan.
2. **Choose your route at Part A, step A7.** The professional route needs Claude Pro, because a free Claude account can't work in your folders; it is the one upgrade the bootcamp is built around. The free route (Step 00, A9) needs no subscription: you simulate the paid features and earn fewer points for them, but you can pass every step. You can switch to the professional route at any step.
3. **Keep ChatGPT free** unless a step needs a paid feature, then consider Go before Plus.
4. **Review after a month.** If you keep hitting limits on real work, upgrade. If not, stay where you are.
